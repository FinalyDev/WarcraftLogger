Getting a log
  • Turn on Advanced Combat Logging (System > Network) and type /combatlog before the raid.
  • The file is in World of Warcraft/_classic_era_/Logs/WoWCombatLog.txt (or the folder for your version).
  • Boss fights come from encounter start/end markers. Everything else is trash, split into pulls by gaps in combat.
